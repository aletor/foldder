Bundled camera profiles (DCP)
=============================

Source: Adobe Camera Raw — Adobe Standard profiles (Canon EOS bodies).

IMPORTANT:
- These are Adobe DCP profiles, not RawTherapee community profiles.
- Before redistributing in a commercial SaaS product, verify Adobe license
  terms for bundled Camera Raw profiles with your legal counsel.
- Users may also load their own .dcp files locally in the Lightroom module.

Alternative: generate DCamProf profiles for your priority camera bodies.
